# ImageGen texture prompts

Mode used: **reference-image edit / visual derivation** with the approved Field Manual board as the style reference. The reference controlled material, palette, and composition language; generated plates intentionally contain no logo or text.

## Light field plate

> Create a clean cinematic 16:9 environmental background plate derived from the visual language of the reference: warm aged washi paper, subtle fibers and handling marks, faint technical grid and registration ticks, quiet Japanese field-manual precision. Place a restrained monochrome sumi mountain landscape along the bottom and rising slightly on the right, with a weathered vermilion sun near the far-right edge. Preserve generous empty negative space across the left and center for typography and an animated logo. High-end editorial design, imperfect analog ink, understated, no people, no text, no letters, no logo, no icons, no mockup, no border. 1920×1080 composition.

## Dark field plate

> Create a clean cinematic 16:9 dark environmental background plate derived from the visual language of the reference: deep sumi-black handmade paper, subtle charcoal fibers, worn ink texture, a sparse technical grid, tiny aged-gold registration specks. Add a low restrained indigo-black mountain silhouette along the bottom and one dry vermilion brush arc emerging at the far right. Preserve generous empty negative space across the left and center for typography and an animated logo. High-end Japanese field-manual editorial design, tactile and calm, no people, no text, no letters, no logo, no icons, no mockup, no frame. 1920×1080 composition.

## Usage

- Use these only as atmosphere behind deterministic SVG/logo animation.
- Keep the empty left/center region.
- Reject any generation containing pseudo-letters, invented symbols, or a second sun.
- The untouched ImageGen source outputs are saved in `textures/*-source.png`; normalized 1080p and 4K plates are alongside them.
