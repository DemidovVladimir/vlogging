# Motion brief: TENGU Field Signal

## Deliverables

- Logo sting: 2.4 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 with stereo audio.
- Transparent logo overlay: 2.4 seconds, ProRes 4444 with alpha and stereo audio.
- Chapter bumpers: 3 seconds, light and dark, editable title/category/subtitle.
- Optional vertical versions: 1080×1920 with the same timing and simplified lockup.

## Creative intent

The bumper should feel like a field instrument finding a signal. It starts with human intent, organizes specialist agents, then exposes the route to a verified result. The sound is dry, physical, and precise: node ticks, a short paper/air sweep, and one restrained low stamp.

## Timing

| Frames | Beat | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | Red core stamps in | Soft low transient |
| 8–26 | Nodes arrive sequentially | Six dry ticks |
| 14–40 | Rings and links draw | Quiet paper/air sweep |
| 28–49 | Black/red arcs complete | Sweep resolves |
| 43–62 | TENGU lockup enters | Low stamp + short shimmer |
| 62–72 | Hold | Tail only |

## Constraints

- No continuous rotation.
- No 3D extrusion, glow, neon, lens flare, or particle explosion.
- No text-to-video generation of the mark.
- The center square is always vermilion.
- The verified node and route are aged gold.
- Use overshoot only on the square and nodes; rules and text ease cleanly.

## Edit usage

- Place the opaque MP4 as a full-frame separator.
- Place the alpha MOV above footage for an identity overlay.
- For a chapter transition, cut picture on the first impact and let audio start 2–4 frames before the cut.
- When the dialogue is dense, use the chapter version without the secondary English metadata.
