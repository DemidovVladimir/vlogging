# YouTube production checklist

## Channel setup

- [ ] Upload `exports/png/tengu-avatar-1024.png`.
- [ ] Upload `exports/png/youtube-banner-2560x1440.png` and inspect on TV, desktop, tablet, and phone previews.
- [ ] Upload `exports/png/tengu-watermark-150.png` as the video watermark.
- [ ] Use channel display name `TENGU — практический ИИ`.
- [ ] Put the descriptor and brand promise in the first lines of About.
- [ ] Check naming, handle, domain, and trademark availability before public launch.

## Every episode

- [ ] Choose one of six content categories.
- [ ] Write one thumbnail promise in 3–7 words.
- [ ] Export thumbnail at 1280×720 and inspect at 10% size.
- [ ] Add title card after the cold open.
- [ ] Use chapter bumpers only at major structure changes.
- [ ] Show source, cost, test, and limitation cards where relevant.
- [ ] Reserve the last 20 seconds for the end screen.
- [ ] Check captions, loudness, and mobile legibility.

## Export QA

- [ ] Logo node count and geometry match the master.
- [ ] No title or wordmark is clipped.
- [ ] MP4: 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264, stereo.
- [ ] Transparent master: ProRes 4444 with alpha.
- [ ] Thumbnail is under YouTube's upload limit.
- [ ] No evidence claim appears without a visible source or test.
