# TENGU Field Manual

## 1. The strategic core

TENGU is a practical AI channel for people who want a working result, not a news feed about hype. Every episode should answer four questions:

1. What human goal are we solving?
2. Which agents or tools do the work?
3. What evidence proves that it worked?
4. What remains unsafe, expensive, or uncertain?

The channel's distinctive idea is **human intent at the center of a visible system**. The red square is the person and their goal. The orbiting nodes are specialist agents. The incomplete rings are process and verification. The gold route is a result that survived checking.

## 2. Name and verbal identity

### Public naming

- Full channel name: **TENGU — практический ИИ**
- Short name in graphics: **TENGU**
- Descriptor: **ПРАКТИЧЕСКИЙ ИИ ДЛЯ ЛЮДЕЙ**
- Brand line: **Человек задаёт цель. Агенты работают. Результат проверяется.**

### Tone of voice

- Calm, exact, observant.
- Confident without pretending certainty.
- Technical details are shown when they change the conclusion.
- Results, limits, sources, and costs are visible.
- Avoid “революция”, “магия”, “убийца профессий”, and empty superlatives.

### Episode title grammar

Use a concrete action plus a real tension:

- `Собрал ИИ-агента для почты. Где он сломался`
- `5 агентов делят одну задачу — полный процесс`
- `ИИ сделал работу. Вот доказательства`
- `Автоматизировал монтаж — сколько времени сэкономил`

## 3. Visual idea

The system mixes three layers:

- **Field manual:** grids, coordinates, labels, chapter numbers, registration marks.
- **Japanese materiality:** washi, sumi, vermilion, seal logic, empty space.
- **Agent systems:** nodes, incomplete orbits, routes, status markers, verification points.

This should feel like an intelligent object someone has kept, annotated, and used—not a generic “AI neon” brand.

## 4. Logo system

### Meaning

- Red square: human intent, the non-negotiable center.
- Six nodes: specialist agents or sources.
- Black arcs: process still in motion.
- Red brush arc: signal, attention, decision.
- Gold node and route: verified outcome.
- Crosshairs: measurement and accountability.

### Versions

- `tengu-mark-primary.svg`: default, editorial and large-size use.
- `tengu-mark-simplified.svg`: avatar, watermark, sizes under 96 px.
- `tengu-mark-inverse.svg`: dark fields.
- `tengu-wordmark-custom.svg`: clean master wordmark drawn as original vector geometry.
- `tengu-wordmark-hero.svg`: heavier distressed wordmark for large title moments.
- `tengu-wordmark-horizontal.svg`: banner, deck, press and partnership use.
- `tengu-wordmark-stacked.svg`: square or portrait fields.
- `tengu-stamp.svg`: an accent, never the only identifier.

### Rules

- Clear space: at least the width of the central red square on every side.
- Minimum digital size: primary mark 96 px; simplified mark 24 px.
- Never rotate the full mark, change node count, replace the red square, add glow, or put it inside a new arbitrary frame.
- On photographic footage, use the inverse version or the supplied lower-third plate.
- The `TENGU` wordmark is already vector geometry and has no font dependency. Convert only the editable descriptor text to outlines for final print delivery.

## 5. Color

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Sumi | `#11110F` | Main ink, dark background |
| Washi | `#E9DFC9` | Main light field |
| Vermilion | `#C92F20` | Human intent, active signal, critical phrase |
| Indigo | `#1D3342` | Technical depth, secondary panels |
| Aged gold | `#A9853E` | Verified outcome, evidence, metadata |

Target contrast: body copy should use Sumi on Washi or Washi on Sumi. Vermilion and gold are accents, not paragraph colors.

## 6. Typography

- Brand wordmark: **custom TENGU lettering**, used only for the name.
- Display and thumbnails: **Manrope ExtraBold / Bold**.
- Metadata, chapter labels, sources: **IBM Plex Mono SemiBold**.
- Optional editorial quotations: **Literata**.
- Optional Japanese accent: **Shippori Mincho**.

Use uppercase for short labels and metadata. Russian episode titles may use uppercase, but keep them to 3–7 words on thumbnails. Do not imitate the custom wordmark in body text and do not letter-space long Cyrillic text excessively.

## 7. Content taxonomy

| No. | Category | Promise | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Идеи | Decide what matters | Concepts, opinion, framing |
| 02 | Агенты | Show who does the work | Agent roles, orchestration |
| 03 | Инструменты | Set up and use | Software, models, workflows |
| 04 | Практика | Build, test, improve | Tutorials, projects, automations |
| 05 | Доказательства | Show results honestly | Benchmarks, failures, audits |
| 06 | Люди | Connect AI to real life | Work, family, communication |

The category marker appears in the top-left metadata line. It is a navigation aid, not decorative filler.

## 8. YouTube application

### Banner

Master size is 2560×1440. Keep the name, mark, and descriptor inside the centered 1546×423 safe area. The dashed guide in the editable SVG is a production guide and can be hidden before final export if preferred.

### Avatar

Use the supplied dark rounded-square avatar. The simplified mark survives the YouTube circular crop. Do not use the full wordmark in the avatar.

### Thumbnails

Fixed grammar:

1. Category line at top left.
2. One promise or conflict in 3–7 words.
3. Mark or subject on the opposite side.
4. One vermilion intervention: line, circle, square, or phrase.
5. No more than one photo plus one symbol.

Light template: ideas, tools, tutorials, calmer episodes. Dark template: evidence, failures, security, comparisons, verdicts.

Avoid UI collage, tiny screenshots, more than two type sizes, gradients, neon purple/blue, stock robots, and unverified numeric claims.

### Title card

Use for the opening promise after the cold open. Hold 1.5–2.5 seconds. Keep it quieter than the thumbnail.

### Chapter bumper

Use between major parts, not every minute. Duration: 2.4–3.0 seconds. Audio should bridge across the cut by 2–4 frames so it feels integrated rather than pasted in.

### Lower third

Hold 4–6 seconds. Place on the lower left. Do not cover burned-in subtitles; move subtitles upward or use the opposite side.

### End screen

Reserve the final 20 seconds for YouTube end-screen elements. Use two video targets and one subscribe target. Let the host's spoken CTA name the next specific video.

## 9. Motion grammar

The animation tells the brand story in four beats:

1. **Human:** the red square stamps into place.
2. **Agents:** nodes leave the center and find their roles.
3. **Evidence:** rings and paths draw; the gold route appears.
4. **Outcome:** the system settles and the TENGU wordmark enters.

Motion is mechanical with slight material imperfection. Use short springs for nodes, drawn strokes for routes, and one decisive low impact. Avoid liquid morphing, constant spinning, particle explosions, or camera fly-throughs.

## 10. Image and texture direction

- Prefer wide negative space, mountains low in frame, a single vermilion sun or brush arc.
- Paper and ink can be imperfect; the logo cannot.
- Photographs should feel observed and real: workshop, desk, family, street, field notes, devices in use.
- Grade toward warm paper, neutral skin, deep sumi blacks, restrained red.
- Generative texture plates may be used, but never invent logos, interface claims, or evidence.

## 11. Repeatable episode package

For each episode produce:

- one thumbnail from a light or dark master;
- one title card;
- 2–5 chapter bumpers using the category system;
- lower third for each named person;
- evidence cards for sources, costs, and final verdict;
- end screen;
- a 9:16 crop with simplified mark and readable captions.

## 12. Accessibility and quality control

- Check thumbnail at 10% scale before upload.
- Keep important elements away from all four edges by at least 48 px at 1280×720.
- Use captions and do not communicate a conclusion by color alone.
- Listen to the bumper at phone-speaker volume; the impact must not spike above dialogue.
- Confirm node count, central square, wordmark spelling, title safe zones, and export dimensions before publishing.

## 13. Ownership and naming check

The package establishes a creative direction, not a legal clearance. Before public launch, check channel-name, handle, domain, and relevant trademark availability in the markets where the channel will operate.
