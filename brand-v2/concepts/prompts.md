# ImageGen prompts — TENGU v2

Режим: встроенный ImageGen. Все четыре изображения — новые генерации, не редактирование старого логотипа.

## 01 — Kinetic Mon

> Premium brand identity exploration for a practical AI, safety, open-source and family media brand. Reinterpret a Japanese family crest as a living coordinated system: one calm central void, six unequal agent fragments, controlled motion and one deliberate opening. Flat vector forms, disciplined negative space, hand-cut seal versus precise geometry. Sumi, washi, vermilion and aged gold. Show mark, monochrome, avatar, wordmark TENGU, thumbnail fragment and motion. Avoid triskelion, propeller, recycling, shuriken, gear, literal tengu, cyberpunk and AI clichés.

## 02 — Proof Seal

> Build the brand as a verification system for AI tools, plugins and code. Create an original modern hanko-like glyph from interlocking strokes and negative space, compressing human judgment, evidence and many agents into one mark. Use a broken square/circle boundary, authoritative but slightly imperfect. Show seal, vector reduction, approval stamp, TENGU wordmark, YouTube crop and README. Avoid checkmark, shield, padlock, police badge, QR code, cultural pastiche and literal tengu imagery.

## 03 — Field Manual

> Create an expandable editorial identity, not merely a logo. A small warm square represents the human; offset orbital notation, cropped rings, index marks and one red seal stroke represent agents, evidence and action. Make it feel like an invented Japanese technical field manual: precise but tactile, asymmetric ma, rigorous grid with human irregularity. Show symbol, TENGU wordmark, category markers, title cards, thumbnails and four-frame intro. Avoid dashboards, circuit boards, pills UI, Nick Saraev styling and Japanese decoration for its own sake.

## 04 — Final Hybrid

> Combine the verification authority of Proof Seal with the editorial intelligence of Field Manual. Brand name TENGU; Russian descriptor ИИ НА ПРАКТИКЕ. Human intent is a red central square, six fixed nodes are specialist agents, an incomplete ring is evidence, and one aged-gold route is the verified path. Enclose it in an incomplete square field frame with a registration notch. Show final mark, one-color seal, tiny avatar, wordmark, Russian YouTube title card, categories, README and motion logic. Avoid targets, radar, solar-system diagrams, literal tengu, random kanji, cyberpunk, generic dashboards and cultural cosplay.
