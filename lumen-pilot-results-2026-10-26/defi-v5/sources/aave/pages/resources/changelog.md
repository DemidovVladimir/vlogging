# Changelog

<Changelog date="2026-10-07">

## Aave V4 Base Market

Aave V4 launches on Base. The market deploys an Equities Hub with a MAG7 Spoke, listing USDC and tokenized equities: AAPLc, AMZNc, GOOGLc, METAc, MSFTc, NVDAc, and TSLAc.

</Changelog>

<Changelog date="2026-09-16">

## Aave V4 Arc Market

Aave V4 launches on Arc, Circle's USDC-native Layer 1. The market deploys a Core Hub with Main and Forex Spokes, listing USDC, EURC, cirBTC, and WETH.

</Changelog>

<Changelog date="2026-07-15">

## Aave V4 Avalanche Market

Aave V4 launches on Avalanche, marking the first multi-chain deployment of Aave V4. The Avalanche market introduces a Core Hub with Main, Forex, and AVAX Correlated Spokes.

</Changelog>
