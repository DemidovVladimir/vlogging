## How Earn Vaults Work

Earn Vaults put deposits to work across Kamino lending reserves. Vault shares track each depositor's position as yield accrues.

01

### Deposit

Users deposit assets into a vault.

02

### Earn Vault

The vault allocates deposits across lending reserves.

03

### Redeem

Users redeem shares for their deposit plus earned yield.
