# Loan Agreement

> The agreement depositors sign when they deposit into Institutional Yield

When you deposit into an Institutional Yield vault, you agree to a **Loan Agreement** with the SPV. This agreement governs your legal relationship as a depositor into the product.

The full Loan Agreement is available on request. Please contact [institutions@kamino.com](mailto:institutions@kamino.com) to receive a copy.
