# How to Manage a Multiply Position

> Monitor your leveraged position, adjust leverage, collateral, or debt, and close it out.

Once a Multiply position is open, you can track its performance and adjust or close it at any time, all in one-click transactions.

## Monitor your position

Track your position in the **My Position** panel. Key metrics include:

* **Net value**: the current value of your position after accounting for debt
* **Net APY**: your effective yield after borrow costs
* **Profit/Loss**: how your position has performed since opening
* **LTV**: your current loan-to-value ratio relative to the liquidation threshold

## Adjust your position

You can modify your position at any time. Available actions include:

* **Withdraw**: reduce your position size and return funds to your wallet
* **Adjust leverage**: increase or decrease your leverage multiplier
* **Manage collateral and debt**: manually add or remove collateral, or repay debt directly

![Manage your Multiply position](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/multiply/manage-position.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=0800492eb0a110a90c55e024b6da9f6c)

To make a change, select your action, enter the amount, and confirm the transaction in your wallet.

![Confirm a position action](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/multiply/execute-action.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=ba41efbbaec264434629d88b1070b973)

## Close your position

When you are ready to close, Kamino reverses the opening process: it repays the debt, withdraws the collateral, swaps what is needed, and returns the remaining value to your wallet, all in a single transaction. To close, select **Withdraw** and withdraw the full amount.
