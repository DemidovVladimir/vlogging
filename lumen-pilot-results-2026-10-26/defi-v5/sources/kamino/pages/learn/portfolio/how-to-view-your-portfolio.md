# How to View Your Portfolio

> See all your Kamino positions, rewards, and history for a connected wallet in one place.

The Portfolio page brings your entire Kamino activity into one view: current positions, claimable rewards, transaction history, and swap history for the connected wallet.

## Before you start

Connect the wallet whose activity you want to see. Everything on the Portfolio page reflects the connected wallet and updates when you switch wallets.

## Open your Portfolio

Open the **Portfolio** page in the Kamino app. Once your wallet is connected, the page populates with your data across every Kamino product.

![Kamino Portfolio page overview](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/portfolio/portfolio-data-1.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=672e26efde10966100150db73ef1b7dc)

## Portfolio data

The summary tiles at the top give you an at-a-glance view of your account:

* **Net value** of the connected wallet
* **Fees & Interest** earned over the account's lifetime
* **Claimable Rewards** available now
* **KMNO Staked**
* **Season Rewards**

Below the tiles, the **Net Value** chart plots your portfolio value over time, with 7D, 30D, and 3M ranges. Use the **Display Value In** dropdown in the top right to switch all figures between USD and SOL.

![Display Value In USD or SOL](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/portfolio/portfolio-data-2.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=1234d2f8c04fe59eaec08a5fc499d856)

## Positions

The **Positions Overview** tab lists your active positions across every Kamino product: lending, borrowing, liquidity, Earn, and Multiply. It is the quickest way to see where your assets are deployed. Click any position to open and manage it.

![Positions Overview](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/portfolio/positions.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=59d93cac6330291bbec01537d56d39e2)

## My Rewards

The **My Rewards** tab shows rewards you have earned that are available to claim. Review what is available and claim directly from here.

![My Rewards](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/portfolio/rewards.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=ecae318f7823a51fc2777eef88fb6f1e)

## Transaction History

The **Transaction History** tab is a record of your past Kamino activity for the connected wallet: deposits, withdrawals, borrows, repayments, and other portfolio transactions.

![Transaction History](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/portfolio/transaction-history-1.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=ba1b6ec13f3e656006083d48a8d989f8)

For each entry, use the arrow icon to open the on-chain transaction in a block explorer. From the menu in the top right, you can also **Export CSV** or **Copy** your history.

![Export CSV or Copy from the menu](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/portfolio/transaction-history-2.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=b6d33ad9489510d54779c6dfb1d952bd)

## Swap History

The **Swap History** tab shows your previous swaps for the connected wallet, including the assets involved and the transaction details. Use the arrow icon to open any swap on-chain in a block explorer.

![Swap History](https://mintcdn.com/kamino-3d73a151/PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR/images/learn/portfolio/swap-history.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=PLATQ08CVUwkaQvR\&q=85\&s=6fbf37f5c5068b41417a4dc6103f03dd)
